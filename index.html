Peeling exterior paint
The problem

Snow, ice dams and gutter buildup make exterior paint fail early on New England homes.

Our fix

Thorough prep (wash, scrape, sand, prime) and high-performance coatings that survive New England winters. Backed by our 5-year warranty.

Rotted wood & trim
The problem

What looks like a paint problem is often rotted wood underneath the surface.

Our fix

We repair framing and trim first, then paint. One licensed crew for carpentry and coating, no subcontractors.

Dark, dated rooms
The problem

Old wallpaper and heavy colors from decades past make rooms feel small and dim.

Our fix

Light, low-VOC Benjamin Moore palettes that open up the space. Clean work, no mess, no strong fumes.

Water stains on ceilings
The problem

Stains left behind by old roof leaks during winter storms keep bleeding through.

Our fix

Stain-blocked, primed and repainted the right way, not just covered up to reappear next season.

Lead paint (pre-1978)
The problem

Homes built before 1978 often carry lead-based paint that unsafe sanding spreads.

Our fix

Lead-safe sanding and disposal practices on every older home. Fully licensed & insured.
